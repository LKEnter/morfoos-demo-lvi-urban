# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences:

1. **Homeowners** in the Helsinki metropolitan area and southern Finland dealing with plumbing
   problems (leaks, blocked drains, broken fixtures, heating issues) or planning a bathroom/home
   renovation. Often contacting under time pressure or stress (active leak, no heat). Need: fast
   response, clear trust signals, easy one-tap contact, straightforward pricing expectations.
2. **Housing companies / property managers (taloyhtiöt, isännöitsijät)** who need a reliable
   long-term LVI partner for maintenance and larger renovation projects. Need: reliability,
   professional documentation, proof of track record, a single point of contact for combined
   LVI + renovation work.

## Product Purpose

Marketing/lead-generation website for Pääkaupungin Putkihuolto, a fictional Helsinki-metro-area
LVI (plumbing/HVAC) and renovation contractor used as a Morfoos demo. The site's job is to convert
visitors into emergency calls, quote requests, service inquiries, renovation leads, and
housing-company contacts.

## Positioning

"Vuosien kokemus LVI-alalta" — years of hands-on LVI industry experience is the core trust asset.
One partner covers both routine plumbing/LVI work and larger renovation/housing-company projects,
removing the need to coordinate multiple contractors.

## Operating Context

- Service area: Pääkaupunkiseutu, Uusimaa, Etelä-Suomi (generic demo regions).
- Services: plumbing work, LVI installations, emergency plumbing, leak repairs, drain services,
  heating systems, renovations, housing company projects.
- 24/7 emergency callout is a standing operational capability, not a marketing-only claim.
- Serves both private/residential customers and housing companies/business customers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No CMS content yet beyond this build; site is built directly as React/Next.js components on
  the `@morfoos/core` platform (same stack as sibling demos in this workspace).
- Quote form should collect: name, phone, email, address, service type, description, preferred
  contact time (per brief).
- Demo placeholders: physical address, testimonials, and stock photography are fictional /
  illustrative and must not be treated as a real business.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Pääkaupungin Putkihuolto (fictional demo identity).
- Contact channels (demo placeholders): phone 040 000 0012; email info@paakaupungin-putkihuolto.fi;
  address Esimerkkikatu 1, 00100 Helsinki.
- Brand personality: reliable, professional, experienced, calm, responsive, trustworthy,
  technical, helpful.
- Emotional goal: visitor should think "when something goes wrong with my home, these are the
  people I call" — the site should reduce anxiety around leaks, heating failures, and renovation
  stress.
- Full visual identity (palette, typography, section structure, imagery direction) is specified
  in DESIGN.md.

## Evidence on Hand

- Demo build: stock photography (Unsplash) and illustrative testimonials are used as placeholders.
- Physical address and contact details are fictional placeholders for the normalized demo.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with experience and calm competence, never with "new company" framing.
2. Every section should visibly shorten the path to a phone call or quote request — this is a
   conversion tool, not a brochure.
3. Serve two distinct funnels (homeowner urgency vs. housing-company reliability) without
   forcing either to wade through the other's content.
4. Reduce anxiety: clear pricing/process expectations, visible 24/7 emergency path, real-feeling
   proof (reviews, experience, service area) over generic contractor cliché.
5. Mobile is the primary device for urgent searches — sticky call/quote actions are load-bearing,
   not decorative.
